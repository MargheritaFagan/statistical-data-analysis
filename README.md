# Statistical Data Analysis

Selected academic projects developed during my Master's degree in Statistical Sciences at the University of Bologna.

## R
Statistical learning and predictive modelling, including regularisation, cross-validation, PCR, tree-based methods and random forests.

## Stata
Analysis of European Social Survey data, including data preparation, survey-weighted analysis, visualisation and multinomial logistic regression.

## SAS
Customer data analysis including dataset merging, aggregation, derived variables, descriptive statistics and statistical reporting.
